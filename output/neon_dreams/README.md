## 📝 Synopsis
Dr. Elara Myles, a brilliant but haunted neuroscientist, is sent on a routine maintenance mission to an abandoned research station orbiting Saturn's moon Titan. As she delves into the station’s archives, eerie whispers of a dark experiment begin to haunt her. The station appears more alive than ever, its walls pulsating with strange light and the faint murmur of voices. Dr. Myles discovers that a powerful corporation is controlling the dreams of billions, using the research from this very station to maintain a global state of constant fear and obedience. As she races against time to uncover the truth and prevent a nightmare come true for humanity, she must confront her own inner demons and question what is real and what is implanted. 'Neon Dreams' combines psychological drama with action-packed suspense, set in a neon-drenched noir universe that pushes the boundaries of human perception.

- **Major Marcus Hart** (Antagonist): The station’s former commander, now revealed to be the mastermind behind the dream manipulation experiment. Marcus is a charismatic yet manipulative figure, using his position of power and influence to maintain control over humanity.
- **Dr. Victor Chen** (Supporting): A former station engineer who becomes Elara's ally and confidant. Dr. Chen is haunted by the loss of loved ones in the experiment and shares Elara’s resolve to expose the truth.
