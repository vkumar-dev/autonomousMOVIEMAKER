## 📝 Synopsis
In the scorched sands of an alien desert wasteland, scattered among ruins of old civilizations, a lone figure named Aria wakes up in a state of perpetual night. Surrounded by scrap yards and the eerie glow of neon advertisements, she discovers that her reality is being manipulated by a clandestine corporation known as DreamForge, which holds the power to control people's dreams. As Aria uncovers the sinister plot behind the corporation's influence, she becomes the target of their wrath. With limited resources and increasing paranoia, Aria must race against time to find a way out before the corporation’s grasp consumes her entirely.
