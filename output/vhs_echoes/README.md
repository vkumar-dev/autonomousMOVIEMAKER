## 📝 Synopsis
In a bleak and isolated retro 1980s arcade town, Detective Jack Hawkins is obsessed with finding his missing sister. As he navigates the eerie streets filled with flickering neon signs and nostalgic video games, he discovers hidden passages leading to parallel worlds. Each dimension holds clues that bring them closer but also deeper into an enigma. Jack's journey becomes a race against time as he uncovers a cosmic conspiracy threatening reality itself. With each macro close-up revealing intricate textures and high-contrast visuals, Jack's search takes on epic proportions, blending the past with the unknown.
