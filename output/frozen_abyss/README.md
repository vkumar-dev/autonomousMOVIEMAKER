## 📝 Synopsis
Set in a submerged metropolis under an icy overworld, Detective Kira Novak navigates the neon-lit streets and subterranean canyons of Aquapolis. As natural elements begin to reclaim the city, Kira follows a trail of cryptic messages left by a rival detective who vanished years ago, leading her into a labyrinthine conspiracy involving powerful factions vying for control of the underwater world's resources. Alongside an enigmatic tech-savvy mermaid named Elara, Kira races against time to unravel the mystery and survive the encroaching darkness before it's too late.
