# 🎬 The Arcade Echoes

> **Logline:** In a forgotten 1980s arcade town, a washed-up arcade owner discovers an old computer that brings back his repressed memories of a lost love, blurring the line between past and present.

## 📊 Movie Stats
- **Genre:** Mystery, Drama
- **Total Runtime:** 0.2 minutes
- **Trailer Runtime:** 3 seconds
- **Scene Count:** 1

## 📝 Synopsis
Set in the desolate midwestern landscape of a forgotten 1980s arcade town, 'The Arcade Echoes' follows John Harper, a washed-up arcade owner struggling to stay relevant in a digital age. One day, he discovers an old computer hidden beneath his family's garage. As he plugs it into the town's power grid, memories flood back of his high school sweetheart and her secret love for him. The blurred lines between past and present become increasingly evident as John navigates his memories through a labyrinth of artificial intelligence simulations, grappling with his identity and the impact of his repressed feelings on those around him. With slow tracking shots and wide establishing views, 'The Arcade Echoes' employs gothic expressionism and monochromatic high-contrast to create an atmosphere of eerie unease, while fast-paced action sequences drive the plot forward.

## 🎭 Characters
- **John Harper** (Protagonist): A once-prominent arcade owner in his 50s, John is struggling to adapt to a changing world. His memories of his high school sweetheart, Sarah, and her secret love for him drive much of the plot forward. As he delves deeper into his past through the old computer, John's identity becomes increasingly muddled.
- **Sarah** (Antagonist (in a sense)): John's high school sweetheart who symbolizes the lost love and unrequited feelings that haunt him. Her involvement in the plot through memories and AI simulations serves as both an emotional anchor and a source of conflict for John.
- **Glen** (Supporting): A local teenager who discovers the old computer and helps John access his memories. Glen's curiosity and innocence provide comic relief while also serving to highlight the dangers of delving too deeply into memories.
