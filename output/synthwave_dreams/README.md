## 📝 Synopsis
Astronaut Mia Chen is sent on a routine maintenance mission to an abandoned research station orbiting a distant star. As she descends into the station's eerie corridors, strange phenomena begin to manifest, blurring the lines between dream and reality. Mia discovers that a powerful corporation, known only as 'The Dreamers,' has infiltrated the station and is manipulating everyone's subconscious to maintain control over society. With limited resources and time running out, Mia must race against the clock to uncover the truth before it's too late, all while fighting off the hallucinatory creatures that threaten to destroy her sanity. In a climactic battle through the twisted dreamscape, Mia faces her deepest fears and uncovers the shocking identity of 'The Dreamers,' leading her on an intense journey to expose the conspiracy and free humanity from its grasp.
